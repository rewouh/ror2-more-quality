# MoreQuality

> **Beta, in active development.** Expect balance changes and the occasional bug; reports are very welcome (see Feedback below).

Adds [Quality](https://thunderstore.io/c/riskofrain2/p/Goorakh/Quality/) variants to items Quality doesn't cover. Every Uncommon, Rare, Epic and Legendary version keeps the item's normal effect and gets its own quality bonus, from stronger numbers to brand-new effects.

The mod has two separate parts:

| Part | Status |
|---|---|
| **Starstorm 2** items and equipment | Available (36 pickups) |
| **Hallowed Concepts** Hallowed items and Hexes | Available (25 items) |

## Hallowed Concepts

Quality doesn't cover the DLC's new tiers yet, so MoreQuality adds them. If Quality adds Hallowed Concepts support itself, this part will be removed from MoreQuality in favour of the official version.

Values are Uncommon / Rare / Epic / Legendary.

### How it works

- **Hallowed items** get quality variants with stronger effects and new twists.
- **Hexes** get quality variants that are *worse*. The payoff: every quality Hex drained by a Hallowed Shrine's Hand of Life **guarantees a choice of its quality** among the items it offers. The best tiers go to the best groups first, one per group, so you can actually take them.
- Quality versions only come from the DLC's own sources (Concept Reliquaries, the Hand of Death, Hallowed Shrines, Compulsions, trials), through Quality's normal quality chance. Never from quality chests or printers.

## Notes

- **Multiplayer:** every player needs the mod. Effects run on the host.
- **Config:** rewritten descriptions use Starstorm 2's default values. If you changed Starstorm 2's item config, the effects still use your values, but some descriptions will show the defaults.
- Buff tooltips have proper names and descriptions with [LookingGlass](https://thunderstore.io/c/riskofrain2/p/DropPod/LookingGlass/).

## Known issues

- **Man-o'-War:** the jellyfish looks like a small red orb while it flies (its explosion is the real Jellyfish nova).
- **Nightmare Demon:** when its host dies, a demon sometimes falls to the ground and fades instead of jumping to a nearby enemy.

MOREQUALITY: QUALITY EFFECTS FOR STARSTORM 2 (v3)
=================================================
Versions: Starstorm2 0.6.46, Quality 0.8.5
Status: v3, matches MoreQuality 0.1.0 (published on Thunderstore as Rewouh-MoreQuality).
        36 of the 40 pickups are implemented; the 4 Starstorm2 beta items are not yet.
        This document is kept in sync with the mod: when an effect or a number changes
        in the code, it changes here too.


HOW TO READ THIS
----------------
A quality copy of an item ALWAYS also works as a normal copy (Quality does this for every
item it knows about, and Starstorm2's items read their stacks the same way). The lines below
are only the EXTRA effect each quality tier adds on top.

Tier scaling roughly follows Quality's own curve: Uncommon x1, Rare x2, Epic x3.25,
Legendary x5 (of the Uncommon bonus), with Legendary often getting a special twist.

"(+X per stack)" means per extra copy of that same quality tier, as in Quality.

Tags:
  [CHANGED]  rewritten from your notes (v2)
  [KEPT]     you approved it as is (v2), sometimes with your addition ("+ you")
  [TUNED]    changed after the first in-game test (v3)
  [NOT YET]  designed, but not implemented (Starstorm2 beta item)

Effort: E = easy (a stat hook)
        M = medium (hooks into Starstorm2's own item code)
        H = hard (new projectiles, summons, physics or visuals)

Base numbers are from Starstorm2's default config (SS2.Items.cfg).
Values written "x / y" for a stat that already exists are TOTALS (base + quality),
as the in-game descriptions show them.


IN SCOPE: 40 pickups (36 implemented)
-------------------------------------
  White 14 | Green 10 | Red 9 | Boss 1 | Equipment 6
  Not yet: Ice Tool, Neurotoxin Gland, Sticky Overloader, Toy Soldiers. They only exist
  when Starstorm2's 0.7 beta is enabled, so they can't be registered or tested yet.

Not included: the lunar items (Primal Birthright, 3 Relics, Rock Fruit), Starstorm2's
custom-tier items (Remuneration, Stirring Soul), Relic of Extinction (no tier), elite
aspects, and the 0.7 beta items (Scavenger's Fortune, Bane Flask, etc.), which are
disabled in your config.

=====================================================================
DECISIONS (all resolved)
=====================================================================

A. EMPTY DIARY (#5): rarity is rolled like a small chest; the Diary only sets the
   quality of the item it gives. Starstorm2 consumes the diary by removing the BASE
   item, which would never use up a quality diary, so MoreQuality consumes quality
   diaries itself (same levels, plus the item).

B. MAN-O'-WAR (#19): the jellyfish is a homing orb that looks like the real
   Jellyfish (same model) and explodes on contact. No AI ally.

C. LOW QUALITY SPEAKERS (#20): the mod computes landing damage itself, like vanilla's
   Breaching Fin. Flying enemies take the landing hit at once. Bosses are launched
   too, with landing damage capped at 10% of their max health.

D. INSECTICIDE (#29): numbers kept as written. The poison ticks 4 times per second
   (not 3 as first assumed), so a 3s poison at Legendary has about a 17% chance to
   kill. Bosses are never affected.

E. NEUROTOXIN GLAND (#21): redirected attacks hit the other enemy instantly, with a
   small lightning-style visual so it reads clearly. (Not implemented yet.)

SUMMARY
=====================================================================
  Implemented: 36 of 40 (the 4 beta items are designed but not built)
  Tuned after the first test (v3): Coffee Bag, Guarding Amulet, Low Quality Speakers,
    Back Thruster, Simple Magnet

Descriptions: when quality changes a number Starstorm2 already shows, the whole
description is rewritten with the new value in place, marked with Quality's diamond.
When quality only adds an effect, Starstorm2's own text is kept and the new effect is
added under it.

Buffs added by the mod (named in LookingGlass tooltips): Blood Rush, Stim: Armor,
Stim: Speed, Stim: Damage, Stim: Crit, Stim: Regeneration, Accelerated Field.

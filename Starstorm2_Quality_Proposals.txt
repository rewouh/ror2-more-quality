MOREQUALITY: DESIGN DOCUMENT (v8)
=================================
Mod versions: Starstorm2 0.6.46, Quality 0.8.5, Risk of Rain 2 with Hallowed Concepts
Mod release:  0.3.0 (Thunderstore: Rewouh-MoreQuality), beta

The mod adds Quality tiers to items Quality doesn't cover itself, in two independent parts:

  PART 1  Starstorm 2 items and equipment ............. implemented (36 pickups)
  PART 2  Hallowed Concepts Hallowed items and Hexes ... implemented, being tested

Part 2 is kept separate on purpose: if Quality adds Hallowed Concepts support itself, that
whole part is removed from MoreQuality.

This document is kept in sync with the code: when an effect or a number changes, it changes
here too.


HOW TO READ THIS
----------------
A quality copy of an item always also works as a normal copy, so every line below is the
EXTRA a quality tier adds. Values are Uncommon / Rare / Epic / Legendary.

RULES
  - Every tier has the SAME effects; only the values change. No effect is reserved for a tier.
  - Values climb steeply, roughly x1 / x2 / x5 / x10 of the Uncommon bonus (adapted per
    item), so Epic is strong and Legendary is a bit overpowered on purpose.
  - Quality values stack. "(+X per stack)" means each extra copy of that tier adds X. Values
    without it follow the general rule: the best copy gives its full value and every other
    quality copy adds half of its own tier's value (multipliers: half of the part above x1).
    Lower-is-better values (cooldowns, hits needed, thresholds) use the best copy only.
  - Effects use MoreQuality's own visuals (particles and small models built in code; only the
    base glow, smoke, spark, blood and surface textures come from the game). See VISUALS at
    the end of Part 1.

When quality changes a number the item already has, the value shown is the TOTAL (base +
quality), as the in-game descriptions show it. Equipment has no stacks: only the tier of
the one you hold counts.

WHAT THE DLC ADDS
=================
Two new item tiers:
  - Hexes (14): pure maluses, no upside. They can't be scrapped or printed.
  - Hallowed items (11, purple): very strong items, obtained mainly by getting rid of Hexes.

How Hexes are obtained:
  - Concept Reliquary: a free chest containing a random Hex.
  - Hallowed Shrine, Hand of Death ("fester"): drops 3 Hexes per player (random, equal
    odds); they must be picked up before the next Teleporter starts charging.
  - Anima Harvester fight: each player gets 3 Hexes during its second phase.
  - Some Hexes are temporary; only permanent ones can be cleansed.

How Hallowed items are obtained:
  - Hallowed Shrine, Hand of Life ("cleanse"): appears near the Teleporter once someone has
    held a Hex or an N'tormat Page. It drains up to 7 permanent Hexes and drops a
    "Hallowed Potential" with three groups; you take one option per group:
        Low     ~97% white, ~3% Hallowed
        Medium  ~94% green, ~6% Hallowed
        High    ~99% Hallowed (rarely a Page)
        Max     ~92% red, ~8% boss
    More Hexes drained unlock more options (1 Hex: 3 Low; 5: a High; 7: the Max option).
    The better shrine after the Anima Harvester offers Medium and High options only.
  - Shrine of Compulsion: spawns a Hallowed item that is "Vexed" until its holder
    completes a Compulsion objective.
  - The Winnowing trials (Anima Portal): the Greater Trial Blessing lets you keep one item.
  - The Purify Page of the N'tormat: turns 3 Hexes into an item.


WHERE QUALITY VERSIONS COME FROM
================================
Only from the DLC's own sources above, through Quality's normal chance for a drop to be a
quality item. Quality's quality chests and printers never offer Hexes or Hallowed items
(they draw from the white/green/red/boss/lunar/void/equipment lists only), and Simple
Magnet's upgrade skips both tiers.


THE CLEANSING PAYOFF
====================
A quality Hex is a WORSE Hex. The payoff: every quality Hex drained by the Hand of Life
GUARANTEES one choice of its quality in the Hallowed Potential.
  - The best tiers are placed first, starting with the best group (High/Max), then
    Medium, then Low, one per group per round, so you can actually take them: you pick
    one item per group.
  - With more quality Hexes than groups, the next round fills a second choice per group.
  - Only unlocked choices are upgraded, and only items that have quality versions.
  - A choice that is already of that quality or higher is left alone.
So carrying a Legendary Hex for a few stages guarantees a Legendary choice when you cleanse
it. (With the Artifact of Command the shrine drops items directly; not covered.)

Quality stops item costs (printers, scrappers) from spending quality items. The shrine pays
with hexes through that same cost, so MoreQuality gives the hex cost its own check and
payment that include quality hexes; other item costs keep Quality's rule.

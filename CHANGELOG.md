# Changelog

## 0.3.1

- [Risk of Options](https://thunderstore.io/c/riskofrain2/p/Rune580/Risk_Of_Options/) support (optional): MoreQuality has its own settings page.
- New: **Quality Hand of Death**. A Hallowed Shrine's Hand of Death has a 10% chance (configurable) to be white and glowing; every hex it drops gets its own random quality (40% Uncommon, 30% Rare, 20% Epic, 10% Legendary).

## 0.3.0

- **Every quality tier now has the same effects**; nothing is reserved for Epic or Legendary any more. Values climb steeply (roughly x1 / x2 / x5 / x10), so Legendary is a bit overpowered on purpose.
- **Quality values stack**: extra quality copies add half of their own tier's value (two Uncommon Vengeful Spirits are now better than one).
- **MoreQuality's own visuals**: a flaming molten coin and a chocolate bar model, a fire ring when Molten, a blood wave and blood drops for Sanguine Chalice, a ticking pulse (with sound) for Watch Metronome, toxic clouds, coloured shockwaves, and status marks (Pinned, Hunted, Voodoo, Slowed, Overcharged, Gold Rush, Wounded).
- **Man-o'-War** now releases a real Jellyfish on your team, so it finally looks like one (no more red orb).
- New mechanics for items that only had bigger numbers:
  - Fork, **Pitchfork**: repeated hits pin an enemy in place, and pinned enemies take more damage.
  - Watch Metronome, **On the beat**: hits right after each tick are big crits and can give a charge back.
  - Molten Coin, **Pay to burn**: burning enemies drop molten coins that set enemies around you on fire.
  - Green Chocolate, **Comfort food**: drops a chocolate bar that cleanses and shares the buff with nearby allies.
  - Swift Skateboard, **Grind**: a burning trail at max stacks.
  - Universal Charger, **Overcharge**: the refreshed skill's next use boosts your damage.
  - Hunter's Sigil, **Hunting grounds**: enemies in the zone take a lot more damage.
  - Jet Boots, **Afterburner**: rocket jumps leave a pool of fire.
  - Needles, **Acupuncture**: needled enemies become voodoo dolls that share damage.
  - Portable Reactor, **Meltdown**: blows up when invulnerability ends (replaces the double chest drop).
  - Insecticide, **Pesticide cloud**: poisoned enemies leave a toxic cloud when they die.
  - Field Accelerator, **Accelerated particles**: faster projectiles and slowed enemies in the zone.
  - Galvanic Core: razed enemies arc lightning. Erratic Gadget: strikes can open a chest for free. Droid Head, **Self-destruct**: drones explode.
  - M.I.D.A.S., **Gold rush**; Greater Warbanner, **Plant the flag**.
  - Sanguine Chalice, **Blood offering** (replaces the barrier); Earthbound Vessel, **Grounded**.
  - Hexes: Blindness (unseen enemies hit harder), Famine (enemies steal your lost healing), Pain (wounds), Leaping (random sideways push; you can always jump now).

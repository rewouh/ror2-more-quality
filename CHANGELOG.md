# Changelog

## 0.2.4

- Fixed: from the start of every run, almost every spawned enemy had the purple Hex-Empowered buff (+20% damage and movement speed), even without any items. It is now only given to Mimics from a quality Hex of Mimicry.

## 0.2.3

- Short description now mentions Hallowed Concepts.

## 0.2.2

- Mod page wording.

## 0.2.1

- The mod page now lists every Hallowed item and Hex with its quality effects.

## 0.2.0

- New: Hallowed Concepts support. Quality variants for the 11 Hallowed items and the 14 Hexes. A quality Hex is worse, but cleansing it at a Hallowed Shrine guarantees a choice of its quality.
- Fixed: Coffee Bag's Legendary bean pull, Droid Head's second drone and Empty Diary's level-up failed in 0.1.0.
- Man-o'-War: the jellyfish now explodes with the real Jellyfish electric nova.
- Armed Backpack missiles are 3 times bigger at every tier.
- Coffee Bag (Legendary): beans now fly to you from up to 40m.
- Known issues: Man-o'-War's jellyfish still looks like a red orb in flight; a Nightmare Demon whose host dies sometimes falls to the ground instead of jumping to a new enemy.

## 0.1.0

- First release: Quality variants for 30 Starstorm 2 items and 6 equipment.
